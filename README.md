# Tallkeep for Windows

Installers for [Tallkeep](https://tallkeep.com). Tallkeep on your office computer updates itself
from here; you don't need to download anything by hand.

Newest version: **0.26.35**. Tax % and shipping keep their decimals. A payment can't be more than what's owed. Invoices dated before your books start are marked and left out of what's owed. New documents use today's date in the evening too. Vendor credits can be partly used in Pay Bills. Refunds ask how the money goes back. Receiving lets you pick or add a place.

Running Tallkeep on a Synology, a Linux server or anything with Docker: see [docker/README.md](docker/README.md).
