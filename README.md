# Tallkeep for Windows

Installers for [Tallkeep](https://tallkeep.com). Tallkeep on your office computer updates itself
from here; you don't need to download anything by hand.

Newest version: **0.26.34**. Safer money edits: a paid bill can't be voided until its payment is removed, minus quantities and totals below what's paid are stopped with a plain message, and nothing is half saved. Create invoice on a quote always gives an invoice number. Part-received purchase orders show in Pay Bills, Bills, the vendor page and What we owe. Vendor credits look like credits. New companies start their books today, and you can add a place right in Add stock.

Running Tallkeep on a Synology, a Linux server or anything with Docker: see [docker/README.md](docker/README.md).
